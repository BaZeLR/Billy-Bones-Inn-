# Tavern staffing and care rules

This is the intended roster, not a second live staffing registry. The existing NPC
job assignments remain the authority until recruitment and slot management are
implemented in the tavern's staffing owner.

| Category | Work | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household | Wait tables, clean, cook | Existing tavern jobs |
| Client service | Guest room, gloryhole | Existing service jobs |
| Servants | Maid work and yard work | Five servant positions total |

Servant tasks: clean rooms, remove ashes, make fires, groom horses, do laundry,
boil water, chop logs, and carry firewood. A servant can be assigned tasks from
either side of the house; the five positions are a shared cap, not five for each
task list. Recruitment, wages, and task UI are not specified yet, so this plan
does not create placeholder NPCs or a parallel roster.

## Relationships and care

- A tavern-hall entrance presents at most one entry/daily incident before room
  navigation. A work-hours harassment incident and a missed tailor appointment
  must not play back-to-back on the same entrance.
- A premium transfers actual money from MC to each worker's personal purse.
  Its morale and skill effects are separate from the purse balance.
  Amanda's service earnings use that same purse; older saves transfer her
  former service-savings balance once when her NPC state is repaired.
- If Sandra, Melissa, or Amanda has money from a premium and MC misses their
  tailor appointment, they do not complain in front of the team. They remain
  angry until the existing household apology succeeds. Their clothing promise
  remains outstanding.
- If MC brings someone to an agreed barber appointment without enough money,
  or brings her to the tailor without enough for any unowned dress, the broken
  promise costs that worker 2 trust and 2 friendship and increases her
  resistance by 2. The appointment/request ends so the penalty occurs once.
- Sandra's weekly friendship reward comes from completed chores: five of six
  earns +1, all six earns +2. Amanda and Melissa's resistance falls by the same
  amount. Increased attendance is reported but does not substitute for chores.
- The existing barber visit already improves skin, beauty, and the relevant
  household work skills; do not grant that professional improvement merely for
  promising the appointment.

Liza and Georgette remain client-service workers, not members of the three-person
household apology flow. Their current job assignments and story permissions are
unchanged by this staffing plan.
